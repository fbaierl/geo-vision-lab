# The Chronicles of DuckyDucks and FrogyFrogs: The Earth Incursion Attempt

## Chapter 13: The Great Ascension Project (Year 2021-2022)

**CLASSIFICATION: ALLIANCE EYES ONLY - EYES ONLY**

This document chronicles the most ambitious and controversial operation in DuckyDuck-FrogyFrog history: the attempted incursion into the human realm. For the first time in recorded history, both species united with a single objective—to physically reach Earth and confront the human threat at its source.

**Operation Status:** FAILED  
**Casualties:** 47 DuckyDucks, 23 FrogyFrogs  
**Security Level:** CRITICAL - Do not discuss with junior academy members

---

## Background: The Catalyst Event

### The Human Devastation of Year 2020

The decision to attempt Earth access was triggered by unprecedented human destruction of Quackswamp border regions:

**Documented Incidents (Year 2020):**
- 12 DuckyDucks captured by human wildlife traders (never recovered)
- 3 FrogyFrog breeding pools poisoned by agricultural runoff
- 200 acres of sacred reed forests bulldozed for human housing development
- Aerial drone surveillance detected over Lake Featherside (multiple sightings)

**Joint Council Emergency Session (Year 2020, Month 11, Day 15)**
- Vote: 8-4 in favor of "extraordinary measures"
- Authorized formation of **Project Ascension**
- Budget: 40% of annual Joint Council resources (unprecedented)

## Chapter 14: The Science Behind Ascension

The possibility of reaching Earth was first proposed by **Professor Paddleton Dimensionquack** (DuckyDuck theoretical physicist) and **Archmage Croakella Voidwater** (FrogyFrog dimensional magic specialist).

**The Dimensionquack-Voidwater Hypothesis (Year 2019):**
> "The barrier between Quackswamp and Earth is not absolute. Certain locations—called 'thin places'—have weakened dimensional boundaries. With sufficient magical and technological amplification, physical passage may be achievable."

## Chapter 17: What Happened on Earth

### The Arrival

**Survivor Account: Captain Wingmore Skyhunter**

> "We emerged into darkness. Not the darkness of night, but the darkness of a concrete enclosure. The air was toxic—burning our lungs immediately. We were in some kind of human containment structure."

**Survivor Account: Mage Ribbitus Stormcaller**

> "The water magic that sustains us... it was WRONG there. Corrupted. Twisted. I could barely channel enough power to light a single lily pad."

### The Human Trap

**Post-Mission Analysis:**
- Humans had detected dimensional activity months before incursion
- Facility identified as "Human Wildlife Containment and Research Center"
- Coordinates (estimated): 45.2900° N, 12.4600° E (approximately 2km from Lake Featherside "thin place")
- Humans were WAITING for them

### The Battle of Containment Block 7

**Human Forces:**
- Approximately 40 armed personnel
- Automated defense turrets
- Chemical suppression systems
- Electromagnetic dampening field (nullified FrogyFrog magic)

**Allied Forces:**
- 24 expedition members (12 DuckyDucks, 12 FrogyFrogs)
- Limited magical capability due to dimensional interference
- No heavy weapons (portal mass restrictions)

**Engagement Timeline:**
- T+0:00 - 0:30: Expedition members emerge, disoriented
- T+0:30 - 1:00: Human forces open fire (non-lethal suppression rounds)
- T+1:00 - 2:00: 8 DuckyDucks, 4 FrogyFrogs incapacitated
- T+2:00 - 3:00: Admiral Quackington V orders retreat
- T+3:00 - 4:00: Return beacon malfunction (only 5 members escape)
- T+4:00 - 5:00: Portal collapses, trapping 19 allies on Earth side

### Casualties

**Killed in Action:**
- Admiral Quackington V (DuckyDuck) - Mission Commander
- Archmage Croakella Voidwater (FrogyFrog) - Chief Mage
- Lieutenant Featherstone Quickwing (DuckyDuck) - Scout

**Survivors:**

- All suffered severe psychological trauma

## Chapter 18: The Aftermath

### Immediate Consequences (Year 2022, Months 2-6)

**Security Measures:**
- Ancient Quackalot site sealed (permanent guard rotation)
- All thin place locations classified
- Project Ascension records sealed (Level 10 clearance only)
- Survivors debriefed and placed under protective custody

**Political Fallout:**
- Joint Council confidence vote: 52% approval (lowest in history)
- 3 Council members resigned in disgrace
- Admiral Quackington V posthumously stripped of rank (later reinstated Year 2024)
- Archmage Voidwater's family demanded inquiry (ongoing)

**Public Knowledge:**
- Official story: "Training accident during joint military exercises"
- Casualty families told: "Died serving the Alliance"
- Truth known only to: Joint Council (12 members), Project leadership (5 members), Security Chief (1 member)

### Long-Term Impact (Year 2022-2025)

**Strategic Doctrine Changes:**
- Earth incursion permanently banned (Joint Council Resolution 2022-07)
- Defensive posture adopted for all human relations
- Focus shifted to camouflage and concealment technologies
- Purist movement used incident as propaganda ("The Council sacrificed our best for impossible dreams")

**Technological Setbacks:**
- Dimensional research moratorium (indefinite)
- Quackgate technology classified as "Weapon of Mass Destruction"
- 40% of project budget lost with no tangible return
- Economic crisis of Year 2024 partially attributed to Ascension costs

**Psychological Impact:**
- "Earth Trauma" recognized as medical condition
- 23% of surviving personnel developed severe anxiety disorders
- Recruitment for joint operations dropped 60%
- Trust between species temporarily damaged (some DuckyDucks blamed FrogyFrog magic failure)

## Chapter 20: Lessons Learned

### Official Joint Council Assessment (Year 2023)

**What Went Wrong:**
1. Underestimated human technological capability
2. Overestimated dimensional magic reliability
3. Failed to account for dimensional environmental differences
4. Security breach allowed human preparation
5. No contingency for extended Earth presence

**What We Learned:**
1. Humans are aware of Quackswamp existence (at least some factions)
2. Human technology can neutralize FrogyFrog magic
3. Dimensional travel is theoretically possible but practically infeasible
4. Earth environment is hostile to both species (toxic atmosphere, corrupted water)
5. Unity between DuckyDucks and FrogyFrogs remains strong despite failure

### Recommendations (Classified)

1. **NEVER** attempt Earth incursion again
2. Increase surveillance of human activities near thin places
3. Develop contingency for human invasion OF Quackswamp
4. Maintain fiction that Project Ascension was "training accident"
5. Monitor captured personnel families for Purist recruitment attempts
